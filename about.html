History of University College: Key Events

Venture by Basel Mission

With the arrival of colonial modernity, English education challenged traditional Indian education. In Mangalore, alongside British efforts, the German Basel Mission significantly contributed to both the economy and education.

Start of Provincial School

In 1824, Basel Mission started Mangalore's first English medium school, the Provincial School. Though initially met with mixed reactions, locals soon realized the value of modern education. A committee raised Rs. 65,000 to develop the school. By 1866, the government sanctioned classes from standard 1 to Matriculation.

F.A. Classes and Government College Designation

The school introduced F.A. courses in 1879 and acquired its own building in 1870 under Head Master Mr. Ormsby. The campus improved with facilities and curriculum reforms, including classical languages. In 1879, it became Government College, Mangalore, with lower classes moved to a municipal middle school.

Bachelor Degree Efforts and Growth

Attempts to start Bachelor Degree classes began in the 1870s but were initially rejected. Meanwhile, a reading room and newspaper club were formed to improve quality. By the late 19th century, Indian graduates began to lead as Head Masters, replacing Europeans.

Admission of Lady Students

In 1902, women were admitted for the first time, increasing enrollment and activities. The college celebrated its first Annual Day in 1905 and expanded facilities and associations, including dramatic and scientific societies.

Leadership and Development

Principal G.K. Chettur (from 1923) enhanced the college’s academic reputation by introducing associations, a student cooperative, and publications like The College Times and Government College Miscellany.

First Grade College Status

Post-Independence in 1948, the college became a first-grade institution with new degree courses. Affiliations shifted from Madras to Karnataka and Mysore Universities. Science and arts subjects expanded, and new blocks were built in the 1960s and 1970s.

Constituent College Era

In 1993, the college became a constituent of Mangalore University, leading to further growth. New subjects like Microbiology, Computer Science, Business Management, and Journalism were introduced. Library and infrastructure improvements followed, with support from UGC and government.

Continuing Progress

The college continues to expand academically and physically, supported by stakeholders and government bodies, maintaining its heritage and commitment to educational excellence.
