Sports for Everyone

volleyball

Volleyball is a net-based, non-contact team sport played by two teams of six players each. The goal is to hit the ball over a net and land it on the opponent's side of the court without it touching the ground, scoring a point. Each team has a maximum of three touches to return the ball before it must go over the net.

kabaddi

Kabaddi is a team sport that requires seven players in two teams. The aim of the sport is for a single player on offense to run into the opposing team's court up to an extent and then tag out as many defenders of the opponent team as possible and return to the respective court anyhow without getting touched.

Cricket

Cricket is a bat-and-ball team sport played between two teams of eleven players on a large, grassy field. The core of the game revolves around one team (the batsmen) attempting to score runs by hitting a ball and running between two wickets, while the other team (the fielders) tries to prevent them from scoring by taking wickets or catching the ball.

Chess

Chess is a two-player abstract strategy board game where players take turns moving pieces across a checkered board, with the goal of checkmating the opponent's king. Each player starts with 16 pieces, including pawns, knights, bishops, rooks, a queen, and a king, each with unique movement patterns.

Football

A football game, also known as association football or soccer, is a sport played between two teams of 11 players, each aiming to score by kicking or heading a ball into the opposing team's goal. The team with the most goals at the end wins. Football is a globally popular sport with various forms, including American football, rugby, and Australian rules football.
